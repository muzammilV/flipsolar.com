Here’s what to expect

Thanks — we’ve received your request. Here’s what happens next:

  1. We match you. We pass your request to up to 3 local commercial solar installers in your area.
  2. Installers reach out. They may contact you directly by email or phone. Timing varies by installer.
  3. You compare, no pressure. Review the proposals at your own pace. There’s no obligation to choose any of them.

To get better quotes

  • Have your last 12 months of electricity bills handy — installers will ask for them.
  • Ask each installer about licensing, insurance, warranties and the timeline for the 30% federal tax credit, which currently requires systems that did not start construction by July 4, 2026 to be in service by December 31, 2027.
  • Talk to a tax professional about the credit and depreciation.
